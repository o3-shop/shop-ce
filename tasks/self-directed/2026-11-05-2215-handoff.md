## Decisions made without you (on #274)

- **Metapackage keeps exact module pins, not `~X.Y.0`** (changes the plan we agreed).
  - A `~X.Y.0` pin is never rewritten by `ConstraintUpdater`, and `TagFromConstraint` reads
    it as `vX.Y.0`.
  - The module's own `"o3-shop/shop-ce": "~X.Y.0"` does the line selection.
  - Reverse by changing the metapackage pins.
